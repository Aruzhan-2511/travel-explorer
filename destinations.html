Bora Bora

Bora Bora is a tropical island known for its turquoise lagoon and beautiful beaches.

Places to Visit

  • Mount Otemanu
  • Bora Bora Lagoon

Bora Bora is ideal for lagoon excursions, snorkeling, and enjoying island scenery.

Netherlands

The Netherlands is famous for canals, cycling routes, windmills, and tulips.

Places to Visit

  • Amsterdam Canals
  • Keukenhof Gardens
  • Zaanse Schans

Spring is especially popular for seeing tulip fields and colorful flower gardens.

Switzerland

Switzerland offers Alpine landscapes, lakes, scenic railways, and mountain villages.

Places to Visit

  • Interlaken
  • Lake Geneva
  • Jungfraujoch

Paris

Paris is known for its art, architecture, museums, cafes, and romantic atmosphere.

Places to Visit

  • Eiffel Tower
  • Louvre Museum
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral

Walking along the Seine is a wonderful way to explore the city's historic center.

Istanbul

Istanbul brings together historic landmarks, lively markets, and cultures from two continents.

Places to Visit

  • Hagia Sophia
  • Blue Mosque
  • Grand Bazaar

Dubai

Places to Visit

  • Burj Khalifa
  • Dubai Marina
  • Desert Safari
